# International Morse messages from the granite slabs (sandwiched copper
# sheets) installed in the Kryptos courtyard at Langley.
#
# Each line is one engraved message, as letters. Note the deliberate
# misspelling of DIGETAL INTERPRETATIT (Sanborn-confirmed).

# These almost certainly are NOT direct cipher source material -- the letter
# distribution (e.g. 14 I's vs 4 in K4) doesn't match -- but the messages
# read as thematic clues to the narrative context.
